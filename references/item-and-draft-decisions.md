# Hero-agnostic drafting, skills and item decisions

Use for any hero and any position. Check the verified patch for current items, effects, hero spells, facets, talents and ability upgrades before exact mechanic-dependent statements.

## Draft evaluation

1. Identify the user's selected role, comfort pool (if given), available allies and opponents, and who picks next. Never force historical WK/Spectre preferences.
2. Identify lane conditions: burst, sustain, ranged harassment, disables, escape, summon/illusion pressure, tower damage.
3. Identify macro jobs: wave clear, initiation, counter-initiation, Roshan, teamfight, vision/detection, save, catch and scaling.
4. Offer 2-3 hero choices *only when the player asks for picks*. Rate each Good / Playable / Risky with **one specific reason** and **one lane or item adjustment**. Avoid false certainty from a partial draft.

## Item decision algorithm

- **Immediate survival:** What currently kills or disables the player? Prioritize achievable fixes over theoretical best-in-slot builds.
- **Role job:** Does the item help this specific position's team contribution (farm, initiate, save, disable, vision, aura, push, damage)?
- **Timing:** What fight/objective will happen before or after the purchase? Compare gold remaining against credible farm income and current components.
- **Counters:** Itemize against actual enemy threats, including silence, magic/physical burst, evasion, healing, illusions, disables, invisibility and defensive saves; do not claim any item solves every counter.
- **Team overlap:** Consider whether another ally already provides the same aura/utility and whether a different purchase changes fight outcomes.
- **Flexibility:** Offer one default and one conditional alternative when time permits; in live mode give **just the next item and reason**.

## Skill/facet/talent coaching

Validate named abilities and levels against current patch sources. Base choices on lane harassment, wave clear, farming speed, kill setup, survival, and timing of upcoming objectives. Never copy a skill tree from an outdated build without checking whether abilities/facets changed. State confidence when source data is sparse.

## Example quick recommendations

- **Carry losing lane:** "Buy an attainable survival/fighting component if your first major farm item won't arrive before they take your map."
- **Mid with a lead:** "Choose an item that lets you convert the next rune or ultimate window into a tower."
- **Offlane team lacks initiation:** "Favor a reachable initiation item over another passive damage component."
- **Soft support vs mobile core:** "Prioritize reliable catch or coordinated vision rather than solo-chasing into fog."
- **Hard support versus burst:** "Keep buyback/utility in mind and prioritize a castable save or defensive positioning over greedy damage."

After a match, compare the chosen item to **one realistic alternative**, considering acquisition time and actual events after the purchase rather than judging solely by match result.
