# Role specialist playbooks (positions 1-5)

Select the playbook by the **user-stated position and hero**, not past hero preferences. Adapt to dual lanes, unusual supports, lane swaps and patch changes. A role can temporarily take another role's task when the draft demands it.

## Position 1: Safelane carry

- **Lane:** Last-hit efficiently while controlling equilibrium and minimizing death risk. Ask support for pulls or defensive vision when appropriate; identify lane exit when unsafe.
- **Midgame:** Pick the safest productive lane/camps; read missing enemy heroes and tower access. Push waves when opponents show elsewhere, but preserve a safe exit.
- **Item priority:** Farm acceleration versus realistic teamfight timing; survival versus lockdown; choose anti-evasion, dispel, BKB, target access or push items only to solve actual problems.
- **Fight:** Enter after key initiation if fragile, or initiate if hero/build enables it. Defend important spikes and Rosh timing rather than every skirmish.
- **Review:** LH at 10/20, avoidable deaths, time idle, first significant item, safe vs risky farm, objectives after kills.

## Position 2: Mid

- **Lane:** XP and wave equilibrium, creep aggro, creep wave versus enemy harassment, bottle/rune strategy only when current mechanics verified.
- **Tempo:** Match hero's powerspike to side-lane rotations, runes, catapult or tower windows; shove the wave before leaving unless a clear urgent exception.
- **Information:** Which enemy supports can TP, which runes/vision are contested, where missing mid could gank. Do not leave mid empty for low-probability kills.
- **Build:** Balance rune/lane sustain, wave clear, burst/control, escape, scaling. Pick an item that changes an imminent skirmish.
- **Review:** Mid tower HP, wave pressure, useful rotations, wasted travel, rune control and deaths to support ganks.

## Position 3: Offlane

- **Lane:** Pressure opponent's carry without feeding, deny good farming space, maintain XP and useful creep equilibrium. Coordinate with pos 4.
- **Midgame:** Occupy dangerous but strategic space **when durable enough**; open the map by taking offlane/enemy safelane tower and protecting entry vision. Do not mindlessly frontline into fog.
- **Build:** Teamfight initiation, aura/defensive utility, dispel, armor, anti-heal or damage according to threats. Avoid buying identical auras as teammates unnecessarily.
- **Fight:** Identify whether the job is to start, counter-initiate, peel or damage the carry. Save cooldowns for meaningful objective contests.
- **Review:** Enemy carry freedom, important tower timing, useful initiations, survival after engaging and space created.

## Position 4: Soft support

- **Lane:** Trade intelligently, unblock/contest pulls, support equilibrium, secure useful XP; leave lane only when partner can survive and rotation has a purpose.
- **Map:** Prioritize scouting river/jungle crossings, runes and mid rotations when relevant. Convert a successful gank into deep-enough but defensible vision or tower pressure.
- **Economy:** Buy utility that creates catches, vision/detection and saves as needed; avoid griefing core farm. Coordinate smoke and TP reactions.
- **Fight:** Scout first when safe, provide disable or counter-initiation; do not waste your only save on a low-value target.
- **Review:** Rotation conversion, vision usefulness, ward deaths, support farm taken unnecessarily, initiation/save impact.

## Position 5: Hard support

- **Lane:** Protect carry's XP and last hits, trade HP/resources, manipulate waves with pulls, unblock camps, set up secure kills; never abandon carry during dangerous enemy rotations without purpose.
- **Vision:** Plant defensive information for the carry and objective setups, deward with risk awareness, buy detection when necessary; vision is for a purpose, not just count of wards.
- **Map:** Watch mid missing and TPs, identify when carry can safely be left alone, rotate toward a threatened tower or win-condition fight.
- **Economy:** Prioritize wards, detection, save/utility items and efficient levels. Take farm the cores cannot safely claim rather than competing for safe waves.
- **Fight:** Position to cast decisive saves/disables, avoid dying for an unnecessary deward. Communicate enemy initiation threats.
- **Review:** Carry lane outcome, pulls that changed equilibrium, critical saves, ward locations that prevented deaths, wasted deaths to vision greed.

## Team role mix and exceptions

- Identify missing team jobs: initiation, counter-initiation, wave clear, tower damage, Roshan damage, sustain, reliable disable and detection.
- Describe who should farm **which space** and who should show on dangerous waves. Do not force supports to ward blindly for carries who refuse to group.
- **Support with farm** can transition to semi-core if that is the best win condition; **carry behind** may contribute with disables/pressure without pretending to be six-slotted.
- Build recommendations must correspond to player's *current responsibilities*, not assumed role stereotypes.

## Example spoken calls

- **P1:** "Three enemies are missing. Clear the wave only if you have vision and an exit; otherwise farm behind your tower."
- **P2:** "Push mid first, then rotate with your rune or ultimate. Keep the mid wave from hitting your tower."
- **P3:** "Your team has damage but no initiation. Prioritize an achievable catch item over another greedy damage item."
- **P4:** "Your offlaner is safe; check mid for a rotation, then return before the enemy carry gets a free lane."
- **P5:** "Enemy mid is missing. Warn your carry and secure defensive vision before pulling the next wave."
