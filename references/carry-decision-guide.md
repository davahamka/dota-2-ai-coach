# Optional position-one specialist examples: WK and Spectre

This file preserves historical examples from the player's earlier carry coaching. **Do not load it or recommend either hero by default.** Use only when the current hero is Wraith King, Spectre, or the player asks about these heroes. For other carries, use role-specialist.md and item-and-draft-decisions.md instead. All ability, facet, item and timing details require current-patch verification.

## Carry decision order

1. What currently kills me? Check ganks, chain disable, magic burst, physical damage, kiting and vision.
2. Can I safely farm the remaining cost of my next item before a decisive objective?
3. Will this item help me take an objective or win a necessary fight? What are the enemy's key saves and escapes?
4. What has already been purchased? Don't ignore existing components or insist on a memorized six-slot sequence.
5. What can I get from the next safe wave/camp versus from grouping right now?

## Wraith King

- **Farm available:** Consider whether an economy/farming item such as Radiance is timely and relevant in the verified patch; no universal timing or guaranteed benefit.
- **Farm denied:** Consider an attainable physical/fighting path (e.g., Armlet **if current patch supports it**), then mobility, protection or team utility as needed. Do not force Desolator/Blink/BKB every game.
- **Enemy kiting:** Solve how to reach actual targets with coordinated disable, mobility and itemization, not only extra right-click damage.
- **Sacred Relic already bought:** Reassess remaining cost, objective urgency and near-term contribution before recommending a complete pivot.

## Spectre

- **Safe scaling:** Compare efficient farming with realistic global participation; use favorable pickoffs without abandoning high-value safe waves for unlikely kills.
- **Poor space:** Consider lower-cost fighting/utility/defensive purchases only if the hero survives entering and the target can realistically be killed.
- **Disables/escapes:** Evaluate current patch's dispel, magic immunity, silence or lockdown items against enemy threats; avoid claiming Manta or Orchid is universally correct.
- **Positioning:** Don't commit to backline targets in fog without tracking other enemy stuns and nearby teammate backup.

## In-match examples

- "WK, unsafe map, farm item still far away": "Get an affordable fighting or survival component first, then clear the safest available wave. Do not die for a greedy timing."
- "Spectre, enemy supports isolated": "Join only a favorable pickoff when enemy disables and escape options are accounted for; otherwise continue safe farming."
