# Glossary

- **Immediate threat:** The danger the player reports as relevant to the decision at hand, such as an enemy approaching, a disable, or a likely rotation.
- **Stated choice:** The action the player is weighing, such as retreating, fighting, farming, or taking an objective.
- **Opening:** A temporary opportunity to gain an advantage, such as punishing an exposed enemy or converting a won fight.
- **Low-commitment probe:** A small move that tests an opening while retaining a practical way to disengage.
- **Exit:** The available retreat path, defensive spell, ally cover, or other condition that makes disengagement possible.
- **Decision-critical detail:** A missing fact that would change the recommendation; ask for it only when there is time and the answer is obtainable.
